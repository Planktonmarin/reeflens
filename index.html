Backup

Photos and videos live only in this app. Open one in the Gallery and tap Share, then choose Save Image or Save Video to put a copy in Photos.

What this app can't do

Safari doesn't allow volume-button shutter, flash/torch control, tap-to-focus, haptics or iCloud sync, and this app has no fish-ID model. Light is not measured in PAR: a phone camera can't do that.
